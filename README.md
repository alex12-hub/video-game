# video-game

Open Source Looking for someone to finish my browser-based psychological horror game(HTML/CSS/Three.js)

Corps du post :

Hey everyone,

I’m currently an IT student working on a browser-based psychological horror game and ARG built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and Three.js. I’ve reached a point where I can’t take it any further on my own, so I’m releasing what I have and looking for anyone interested in picking it up, remixing it, or finishing it.

The Concept & Story
The game starts as a seemingly normal, minimal web interface—a simple chess game—that slowly corrupts and breaks the fourth wall as you play.

    Act 1 (The Corruption): The chess interface glitches out, pulling the player into a claustrophobic labyrinth where they are hunted by a humanoid entity with glowing red eyes.

    Act 2 (The Cabin & The Forest): Escaping the maze leads to an isolated cabin in a dark forest, transitioning into a non-procedural 3D forest level (built in Three.js) where the player must explore and collect six hidden pages while being stalked.

    The Lore / Ending I had in mind: the pages is a part of protagonist mind in the final act the player is follow by is wife and son.

Limitations & Why I'm handing it over

    Technical limits: Keeping everything lightweight in a single-file/browser environment with Three.js without hurting performance (especially lighting, fog, and collision detection in the forest).

    Asset & Sound design: I’m mainly a coder/student, so creating custom 3D models, animations, and a full atmospheric soundscape from scratch became a major bottleneck.

    Time: Between my IT studies and other commitments, I no longer have the bandwidth to give this project the finish it deserves.

Feel free to fork it, tear the code apart, or take the story in your own direction. If anyone decides to work on it, I’d love to see what you come up with!
